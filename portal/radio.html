Radio pairing

Prepare a logger and receiver for an encrypted local connection. No internet or router is needed once paired.

1. Identify the boards

Close other serial monitors. Connect each board in turn to read its identity. Connecting does not change pairing.

No board connected.

2. Prepare or restore pairing

Preparing again creates new keys. Use the same recovery kit for both boards.

3. Apply and verify

No pairing kit prepared.

Stop recording before changing pairing. The connected board must match the kit’s identity and role. Apply to each board, reboot it, and reconnect to verify status. Pairing is complete when the receiver shows live readings.

Recover the radio

Restart only the connected board’s radio. This keeps the logger running and preserves its recording session.

Connect a board to begin. Use current radio firmware on both boards.
